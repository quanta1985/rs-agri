## 16.3 This handbook and the FAO (2017) handbook

This handbook is developed from the *Handbook on Remote Sensing for Agricultural Statistics* (FAO, 2017) of the Global Strategy to improve Agricultural and Rural Statistics (GSARS). The text is rewritten for the two Vietnamese deltas, not translated; tables and figures taken from the book cite FAO. Part C (environment) is mostly new material that the book does not cover.

| Chapter of this handbook | Corresponding part of FAO (2017) |
|---|---|
| 1 Two deltas | Overview |
| 2 Data | Sections 1.2, 1.4, 1.5, 9.1, 9.3 |
| 3 Land cover | Chapter 2: Land cover mapping and monitoring |
| 4 Rice | Sections 2.5.3, 5.2.1, 5.2.3, 5.2.5 and the annex on the bias of pixel counting |
| 5 Area | Chapter 3 (sampling frames) and Chapter 5 (crop area estimation) |
| 6 Yield | Chapter 6: Early Warning Systems and crop yield estimation |
| 9 Drought | Sections 6.2, 6.4.2 |
| 11 Soil & subsidence | Section 1.4, Table 2 |
| 14 Risk | Sections 6.2, 6.3 |

The handbook's content is licensed [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Remote sensing basics (spectra, indices, radar, classification, pre-processing) are in the sister app [GIS and Remote Sensing Knowledge](https://quanta1985.github.io/rs-knowledge/).
